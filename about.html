About Me

I have been playing tennis for over 8 years, and it has become one of the biggest parts of my life. I love everything about the sport: the competition, the hard work, the friendships, and the feeling of finally nailing a shot you have been practicing for weeks.

I currently play on the tennis team at Prosper High School, and I have been part of the team for over two years. Being on a competitive team has taught me a lot about discipline, strategy, and staying focused under pressure, and I want to pass that along.

That is why I decided to start giving lessons. I want little kids to get the same love for tennis that I had when I first picked up a racket. Whether a kid is just starting out or already has a few years of experience, my goal is to make sure they have fun while building real skills.

I am still in high school, and I really enjoy teaching and working with kids. I plan to keep doing this as I continue through the school year, balancing my schoolwork with lessons so I can give every student the time and attention they deserve.
